PASSIVE VOICE — PRACTICE WORKSHEET

23 exercises · 231 marks · the object moves to the front; the main verb always stays in past participle (V3) — only the auxiliary carries the tense
Tense / FormPassive structureExample
Present IndefiniteObject + am/is/are + V(P.P.) + (by + Sub)The letter is written.
Past IndefiniteObject + was/were + V(P.P.) + (by + Sub)The letter was written.
Future IndefiniteObject + will be + V(P.P.) + (by + Sub)The letter will be written.
ModalObject + modal + be + V(P.P.) + (by + Sub)The letter must be written.
◆ Four traps — no passive here: Intransitive (no object: They arrived at the station) · Stative/possession (have, fit, resemble, lack, suit) · Subject complement (She is a teacher / He became a doctor) · Reflexive (He hurt himself).
