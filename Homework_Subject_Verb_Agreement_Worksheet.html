SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT

RuleIdea
Core / AND Ruletwo subjects + "and" → plural verb
First Subject Rulealong with / as well as / together with → verb follows the FIRST subject
Nearer Subject Ruleeither...or / neither...nor / or → verb follows the NEARER subject
Collective Nounone unit → singular; members acting separately → plural
Measurement Rulemoney / distance / weight / time as one amount → singular
Plural Form, Singular MeaningMathematics, Physics, Economics, News, Measles, Mumps → singular
Paired Item Rulescissors, trousers, glasses, spectacles alone → plural; "a pair of ___" → singular
One of the Rule"one of the ___" → singular
Gerund / Infinitive Subject-ing or "to + verb" as subject → singular
Fraction Rulefraction/percentage "of" + noun → verb follows that noun
A number of / The number of"a number of" → plural; "the number of" → singular
